CLINIC PATIENT POLICY AND INFORMATION

1. Clinic Overview
The clinic provides general medical consultation, follow-up appointments, and basic healthcare services. Patients can book appointments with available doctors through the clinic appointment system.
The clinic operates from Monday to Saturday. Regular clinic hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sunday.
Patients are encouraged to arrive at least 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment so that registration and verification can be completed before seeing the doctor.


2. Appointment Booking
Patients can book an appointment through the online appointment system. During booking, the patient must provide their full name, phone number, and basic appointment information.
An appointment is considered confirmed only after the booking process has been completed successfully.
Patients should check their appointment date and time after booking. If an appointment does not appear in the patient's appointment list, the patient should contact the clinic administration.


3. Appointment Cancellation
Patients can cancel their appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time.
If a patient cancels an appointment less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment, the clinic may not be able to provide the same appointment slot to another patient.
Patients should cancel appointments as early as possible when they know they cannot attend.

4. Rescheduling Appointments
Patients who cannot attend their scheduled appointment may request a different appointment time.
Rescheduling depends on the availability of the doctor and available appointment slots.
A patient should reschedule an appointment before the original appointment time whenever possible.

5. Doctor Consultation
Doctors provide medical consultation during their scheduled clinic hours.
The standard consultation fee is $50 per appointment unless a different fee has been specified for a particular doctor or service.
The consultation fee must be paid according to the clinic's payment procedure.
Patients should discuss their medical concerns clearly with the doctor and provide relevant information about previous treatments when requested.

6. Patient Identification
Patients must bring a valid identity card or other accepted identification document to their appointment.
The clinic may ask patients to provide identification information during registration.
Patients should make sure that the information provided during registration is accurate.

7. Late Arrival
Patients who arrive late may have to wait until the doctor becomes available.
A late arrival does not guarantee that the original appointment time will still be available
If a patient expects to arrive late, they should contact the clinic as soon as possible.

8. Waiting Time
The estimated waiting time may change depending on the number of patients waiting and the doctor's current appointment progress.
The clinic appointment system may display an estimated waiting time when this information is available.
Patients should understand that estimated waiting times are not guaranteed and may change during the day.

9. Clinic Closure
The clinic is normally open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.
The clinic may close temporarily because of an emergency, maintenance, public holiday, or other operational reason.
When the clinic is closed, patients may not be able to book or attend appointments until services resume.

10. Payments
Patients are responsible for paying the applicable consultation or service fees.
Payment information should be checked carefully before completing a transaction.
If a patient believes that a payment was processed incorrectly, they should contact the clinic administration with the relevant payment information.

11. Patient Information
Patients should provide accurate contact information when creating or updating their profile.
The clinic uses patient information to manage appointments and provide healthcare services.
Patients should update their phone number or other contact information when it changes so that the clinic can communicate important appointment information.

12. Doctor Availability
A doctor's availability depends on the doctor's clinic schedule.
A doctor may temporarily be unavailable even when the clinic itself is open.
Patients should check the appointment system for the doctor's available appointment slots before booking.

13. Appointment Status
An appointment may have different statuses during its lifecycle.
A newly created appointment may be marked as pending or confirmed depending on the booking process.
An appointment can later be completed or cancelled.
Patients should check their appointment status before visiting the clinic.

14. Contacting Administration
Patients should contact clinic administration when they experience problems with appointments, payments, account information, or other clinic services.
When contacting administration about an appointment, patients should provide their appointment information so that the issue can be investigated efficiently.

15. General Patient Responsibility
Patients are responsible for providing accurate information, arriving on time, following appointment procedures, and paying applicable fees.
Patients should read the clinic policies carefully and contact the clinic administration if they are unsure about any procedure.
